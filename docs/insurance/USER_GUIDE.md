# Saarthi Insurance Intelligence — User guide

Open /insurance/guide in the running dashboard. Use Guide & help in the sidebar or the question-mark button from any section.

## Start here

For a presentation, choose Start guided demo for the 14-step walkthrough. For a specific question, use the A–P requirement map below. To explore independently, start at Overview, then select a vehicle or driver.

## Navigation basics

### Move between sections

Use the left sidebar. On a phone, open the menu button at the top left. Guide & help and the question-mark button bring you back here.

### Find a vehicle or driver

Type at least two characters in the top search field. Search by registration, model, name or ID, then select a result. Each table also has its own search field.

### Choose the data you are viewing

The top filters combine dates, region, vehicle type, risk level, vehicle and driver. They remain active when you navigate. Select Filters to reveal region, vehicle, driver and risk selectors on any screen size.

### Open records and return

Select the record name or ID in the first table column. Trips, events and incidents open an evidence drawer; use its close button or Escape. Profiles offer All vehicles or All drivers to return to the list.

### Read charts and tables

Hover over or focus a chart point to see its value. Charts that support filtering say so. Use arrow-marked column headings to sort, page controls for more rows, and horizontal scrolling inside wide tables on a phone.

### Control motion

Use Pause animations in the top bar to pause decorative signal flow and turn off entry transitions. Resume animations restores motion. Your system’s reduced-motion preference is also respected. Alert replay has its own Play and Pause controls.

### Export and share a view

Use Export CSV where available for that page’s scoped records. The browser URL preserves the global filters, so you can bookmark it. Local table searches, selected profile tabs and session review actions are not saved in the URL.

## What is in each section?

### Overview

Location: /insurance/overview

Start with the whole portfolio: vehicles, drivers, mileage, risk and incidents.

1. Choose Full 12 months to see the complete observation window.
2. Use the region, vehicle type or risk filter to narrow the portfolio.
3. Open a vehicle or an available chart drill-down to inspect the underlying records.

Remember: Dashboard totals recalculate from the selected trips.

### Portfolio analytics

Location: /insurance/portfolio

Compare risk, usage, geography and vehicle categories across the insured population.

1. Choose a period and region using the top filters.
2. Compare category, road, terrain and behavioural distributions.
3. Open vehicle intelligence to inspect a particular member of the selected group.

Remember: Use the same date range when comparing groups.

### Vehicle intelligence

Location: /insurance/vehicles

Find the owner, actual assigned drivers, mileage, service records and risk of a vehicle.

1. Search the vehicle table by registration, model, owner or ID; select its registration.
2. Overview contains daily/monthly mileage, longest continuous drive, calendar mileage, maps and usage classification.
3. Drivers shows owner versus other drivers and assignment history. Trips opens GPS and driver evidence.
4. Use Behaviour, Incidents, Challans, Service and Risk profile for the related records.

Remember: Find mileage and driving patterns under the vehicle’s Overview tab.

### Driver intelligence

Location: /insurance/drivers

Understand a driver across all the vehicles they have driven.

1. Search for a driver and select their name.
2. Review mileage and safety in Overview; open Vehicles to see linked vehicles.
3. Inspect Trips, Behaviour, Incidents and Challans, then Rewards and Risk profile.

Remember: Higher risk is worse; higher safety is better.

### Accident intelligence

Location: /insurance/incidents

Investigate incidents using location, speed, severity and front/rear camera evidence.

1. Filter the incident casebook by verification status or severity.
2. Select an incident ID to open the investigation drawer.
3. Review its map, telematics and camera illustrations; follow linked evidence.
4. Close the drawer with its close button or Escape.

Remember: A detected incident and a verified accident (simulated) are different record states. Possible factors do not establish cause.

### Live alerts · demo

Location: /insurance/alerts

Demonstrate a speed or attention warning reaching a driver and receiving a response.

1. Keep Overspeeding selected or choose another replay event type.
2. Choose Next alert to step manually, or Play replay and a replay speed.
3. On the driver card, choose Acknowledge warning, then Simulate safe response.
4. Inspect source event for the underlying evidence. Reset alert replay starts again.

Remember: Replay actions reset when you leave the page or change the global filters. The historical ledger stays separate.

### Camera analysis

Location: /insurance/camera

Explore front, rear and cabin findings for incidents, distraction and possible commercial use.

1. Select Incident context, Driver attention or Usage classification under Analysis task.
2. Inspect the annotated scene, confidence and Interpretation and evidence panel.
3. Choose Inspect linked records or select another analysis from the table.
4. Change Demo review status to Reviewed in demo or More evidence needed.

Remember: The drawings and model findings are scripted. Review status lasts only while this page remains open.

### Risk & underwriting

Location: /insurance/risk

Explain a risk score and compare vehicles in the same category.

1. Select Vehicle to assess and a comparison vehicle.
2. Read the contribution and evidence for each of the twelve risk factors.
3. Open the full profile or choose Simulate improvements.

Remember: The score is an illustrative 0–100 heuristic, not a premium or a prediction of claim probability.

### Decision support

Location: /insurance/decisions

Find cases for underwriting review, safety coaching, maintenance or incentives.

1. Review the suggested actions for the filtered portfolio.
2. Open the supporting vehicle or driver record.
3. Inspect the evidence before discussing an action with the insurer.

Remember: Recommendations support a human reviewer; the demo does not accept, reject or adjudicate insurance.

### Claims outcomes

Location: /insurance/claims

Connect exposure, claims costs and a mock coaching programme to the insurer’s claims objective.

1. Start with Full 12 months so both coaching comparison periods are present.
2. Review claim frequency, paid amounts, reserves and the before/after cohorts.
3. Adjust Assumed claims reduction and Programme cost per driver in the planner.
4. Select a claim in Claims ledger, then Investigate accident evidence.

Remember: Sampled outcomes and planner assumptions are separate. The mock programme does not prove real-world claims reduction.

### Rewards & incentives

Location: /insurance/rewards

Show how safe driving earns points and risky events reduce them.

1. Review the leaderboard and reward eligibility.
2. Open a driver to inspect their points and driving record.
3. Use the customer experience to show the same story from the driver’s perspective.

Remember: Points and suggested insurance benefits are illustrative and have no cash value.

### What-if simulation

Location: /insurance/simulation

Explore how different driving inputs change the illustrative risk and rewards.

1. Select a vehicle to simulate.
2. Adjust speeding, braking, night driving, driver count or annual mileage.
3. Compare the observed baseline with the simulated result.
4. Use the reset control to return to the baseline.

Remember: Sliders leave the underlying trip and claims records unchanged.

### Demo scenarios

Location: /insurance/scenarios

Jump into ten prepared stories without searching for a suitable driver or vehicle.

1. Choose a story such as multiple drivers, commercial use, distraction or accident investigation.
2. Read its source data, insight and suggested action.
3. Open the linked profile or incident to inspect the records.

Remember: Scenarios use the full snapshot. Opening their source profiles clears the global filters.

### Customer experience

Location: /insurance/customer

Present the driver-facing view of safety, journeys, rewards and coaching.

1. Choose a demo driver from the selector.
2. Review their safety score and recommendations in Overview.
3. Open Trips, Behaviour, Incidents or Rewards to explore the personal record.

Remember: This is a demo profile selector, not a signed-in customer account.

### Data & integrations

Location: /insurance/sources

Understand where data comes from, how it is calculated and what is simulated.

1. Review the source registry and the four evidence classes.
2. Read the snapshot dates, methodology and integration explanation.
3. Use the data dictionary export or open the mock integration lab.

Remember: Use this page when someone asks whether a camera, OEM or official traffic source is connected.

### Mock integration lab

Location: /insurance/integrations

Demonstrate data arriving from identity, telemetry, cameras, geography, OEM, traffic and insurance sources.

1. Select a Data source and Successful mock response.
2. Choose Run mock sync and inspect the returned record counts.
3. Choose Simulated timeout and run again to show the error state.
4. Switch back to Successful mock response and choose Retry mock sync.

Remember: The lab reads the full deterministic snapshot. Global portfolio filters do not change adapter payload counts.

## Common questions

### Why is a page empty or a profile missing activity?

Your filters may exclude that vehicle or driver, or the chosen dates may contain no matching trips. Use Reset all filters beside the date controls, or Clear filters on the empty profile. A vehicle and driver filter can also be incompatible.

### How do I start a clean presentation?

Choose Start guided demo. It opens the overview with the full dataset and clears global filters. Use Next step and Previous guided step; Finish tour or the close button exits. You can still explore the page while the tour is open.

### Why does the claims planner say both periods are required?

It needs baseline claims before 1 May 2026 and exposure after that date. Restore Full 12 months and clear restrictive vehicle or driver filters. Some small groups have no baseline claims, so a projection is unavailable.

### Why do claims increase even after mock coaching?

The generator lowers selected event probabilities for a seeded coaching group, but individual sampled outcomes can still rise. The planner uses your assumed reduction separately. Neither result establishes the effectiveness of a real programme.

### What does a risk or safety score mean?

Risk runs from 0 to 100: higher means more risk. Low is below 28, Medium is 28 to below 55, and High is 55 or above. Safety is 100 minus risk, so higher safety is better. These are explainable demo calculations.

### Why are calendar years marked partial?

The recorded window is 1 November 2025 to 31 October 2026: twelve complete rolling months across parts of two calendar years. Calendar totals show those coverage dates. Annualised mileage projects the selected period to 365 days.

### Why can I not acknowledge or resolve an alert?

First use Next alert or Play replay to deliver one. Acknowledge warning must happen before Simulate safe response becomes available. Reset clears the interactive responses, and the historical ledger keeps its original scripted states.

### Is any of this connected to real devices or official records?

All identities, trips, camera illustrations, OEM records, challans, claims and alerts are fictional. Inferred usage and possible accident factors carry uncertainty. No live notification or external sync occurs.
